# Corrections and independent review

This is an independent study of Prism ML's models. We welcome public corrections from any reader. A useful correction identifies the model artifact/revision, runtime commit, task or probe, exact result in question, and a reproducible counterexample or receipt. Issues about scoring can include a minimal prompt and output with private information removed.

We will preserve the dated snapshot and publish substantive corrections in a versioned erratum: what changed, why, which rows or figures are affected, and whether the conclusion changes. We will not silently replace a failed measurement with a new threshold or a differently configured run. The public repository's issue tracker will be the correction channel after publication.

Prism ML did not sponsor or endorse this study. Its published training method and benchmark claims are attributed to its [model card](https://huggingface.co/prism-ml/Ternary-Bonsai-2-27B-gguf) and associated documentation; our measurements and interpretations are separately labeled in the [report](REPORT.md).
